# A working agreement that evolves

The private owner is “How to Work With Randall.md,” identified by the installed local pointer. Keep it outside this shared kit and ultimately in Randall's selected Obsidian vault.

The only initial personal facts are his name, network-engineering background, early AI learning stage, use of Claude/ChatGPT, and a reported GPU server whose specifications are unknown. Do not inherit the author's businesses, voice, machines, schedules, agents, or approval rules.

## Learn from approved work

Ask Randall for two or three relevant examples and the scope in which they may be used. For each, record source ID/path, date/version, ownership and permitted destination. Read only the approved material. Preserve exact examples that matter without copying sensitive originals into general context.

Separate:
- **Confirmed:** Randall directly stated or accepted it.
- **Observed:** A bounded pattern in approved evidence; not yet a global preference.
- **Proposed:** A candidate working rule.
- **Superseded:** Replaced by a newer explicit correction; retain its source pointer.

A preference record needs the rule, source/date, scope, confidence or status, and revisit condition. Do not make a personality judgment from a job title or one terse message. Ask when an inferred rule would affect many future tasks. Explicit corrections can update the owner within existing authorization.

## Save work where it belongs

Each project owns its purpose, decisions, research, proof, and handoff. The working agreement owns shared collaboration preferences. Code owns executable behavior. Credentials stay in their secret store; approved originals retain their own owner. Obsidian links the durable meaning and relevant artifacts.

Capture a lasting decision when it happens: what changed, why, evidence, failed assumptions, next action, source ID, and reuse boundary. If the vault is unavailable, keep an explicit pending-capture record locally and reconcile once it returns. Never call an unsynced copy current server truth.

## Prove learning

Start a fresh chat with a different but related task. It should locate the right source and avoid the recorded error without Randall repeating the correction. Test unknown answers and superseded rules as well as happy paths.

Suggested network example: a draft change omitted out-of-band recovery. Save the correction with its scope. In the next lab change plan, the AI should include the management-access recovery check because it retrieved that lesson.

Measure five useful tasks at a time: accepted outcome, corrections, repeat mistakes, elapsed time, actual usage when available, and ability to resume. Fix the largest observed loss. Do not add a rule or plugin just to make the system appear active.

No automatic background history scanner or model training is installed. Any future schedule requires a scope, owner, deduplication, failure path, budget and stop mechanism.
